
# 🏥 Splash — Smart Hospital Queue & Appointment App

Splash is a digital hospital management and appointment system designed to eliminate long waiting lines, save time, and improve patient experience.

In hospitals, time is more valuable than money. Splash makes healthcare access faster, transparent, and digital.

## 📱 App Workflow

    🧍 Patient opens Splash

    🏥 Selects hospital

    👨‍⚕️ Views doctors, availability & experience

    📅 Books appointment

    💳 Pays consultation fee

    ⏱️ Arrives on time — no long queues

    No waiting. No confusion. Faster service.

## 🧠 Why BiteBuddy?

Hospitals still rely heavily on manual queues, paper slips, and physical waiting lines, which waste both time and energy for patients and staff.

Splash was created to solve this problem by bringing speed, clarity, and digital order to hospital workflows.

This app exists to:

⏳ Reduce long waiting lines in hospitals

📱 Digitize appointments instead of physical queues

🧑‍⚕️ Show real-time doctor availability (present / on leave)

💰 Handle consultation fees digitally

📊 Provide transparency on patient count and waiting time

In critical environments like hospitals, time is more valuable than anything.
Splash helps patients save time, hospitals manage load, and doctors work more efficiently.
